# Limitations

- The MVP handles synthetic assets represented in its DataHub graph.
- Missing lineage or selector mappings fail closed and require human investigation.
- Clean retraining with a rebuilt snapshot is not proof of formal machine unlearning.
- The system does not discover offline or uncataloged assets.
- Evidence hashes are tamper-evident demo controls, not a legal-grade immutable ledger.
- No real personal information should be processed by the demo.
